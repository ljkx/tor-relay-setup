Dear [OFFICER / AGENCY],

Thank you for your inquiry [REFERENCE NUMBER] dated [DATE] about activity
from [IP ADDRESS] at [TIME AND TIME ZONE].

[IP ADDRESS] is a Tor exit relay, [NICKNAME], which I operate as a
volunteer. Tor is a free, open network used by journalists, activists,
businesses, law enforcement agencies and members of the public to protect
their privacy. Each connection passes through three relays chosen at random;
an exit relay such as this one forwards the last step of the connection on
behalf of a Tor user it cannot identify.

The relay does not log the connections it forwards, and the design of Tor
means that this server never learns the original address of a user. I am
therefore unable to provide information identifying the person responsible
for the activity you describe, and the server itself contains no such
information.

You can confirm that [IP ADDRESS] was part of the Tor network at the
reported time with the Tor Project's ExoneraTor service:
https://metrics.torproject.org/exonerator.html?ip=[IP ADDRESS]&timestamp=[YYYY-MM-DD]

General information for law enforcement about Tor is available from the
Tor Project: https://support.torproject.org/abuse/
